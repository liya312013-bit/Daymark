PRIVACY POLICY

Updated 10 November 2026

DAYMARK is a website design concept by FeLiya. This page explains how the public demo handles information.

Demo quote form

When you complete the form, your browser checks the fields and shows a confirmation page. The site does not send or store the values you enter, including your name, email address, optional phone number and system choice. This demo does not send requests to Google Sheets or Telegram. Use sample details when trying it.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted by Vercel. Vercel may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice for details.

External links

If you follow a link to Instagram or another service, that service handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Contact

For questions about the demo, contact FeLiya on Instagram.
