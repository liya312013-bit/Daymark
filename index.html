COMMON QUESTIONS

What should I know before asking for a quote?

A recent electricity bill and a list of what you’d like to keep running during an outage are a good starting point.

How long will my battery last during an outage?

It depends on how much energy the battery has available and how much power your connected devices use.

Can I add a battery to my existing solar system?

Share the make and model of your inverter when requesting a quote so it can be checked for compatibility.
